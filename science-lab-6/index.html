🌙 Moon Phase Guide

The Moon does not make its own light. We see different amounts of its sunlit side as the Moon moves around Earth.

New Moon → Waxing Crescent → First Quarter → Waxing Gibbous → Full Moon. The cycle continues through the waning phases.

📏 Space Scale Check

Jupiter is about 11 times Earth's diameter. Saturn is about 9 times Earth's diameter. Uranus and Neptune are about 4 times Earth's diameter.

Important: diagrams often bring planets closer together than they really are so you can see them. Real solar-system distances are enormous.

🧪 Paper Towel Water Race

Question: Does the type of paper towel change how fast water moves through it?

  • Get 2 different paper towels.
  • Cut equal strips and dip each end into equal cups of water.
  • Start a timer and observe how far the water travels.
  • Record your results in a table.
Remember: Change one variable at a time.
